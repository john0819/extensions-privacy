CopyFree – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 October 2026

CopyFree ("the extension") restores right-click, text selection and copying on web pages, and can copy a selection as Markdown.

Summary

  • CopyFree does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal information or browsing data.
  • It makes no network requests.
  • It has no analytics, ads, trackers or remote code.

What stays on your device

  • Auto-unlock sites: the list of websites you chose for "Always unlock on this site". Chrome keeps these on your device as registered content scripts, and you can remove them from the popup at any time.
  • Copied text: text you copy, including Markdown, goes only to your local clipboard.

Permissions

  • activeTab and scripting: run the unlock or Markdown script on the current tab, only after you click the button, the menu item or the keyboard shortcut.
  • contextMenus: add CopyFree's items to the right-click menu.
  • Optional site access: requested for one site at a time, only when you turn on auto-unlock for it. You can revoke it in chrome://extensions.

Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
